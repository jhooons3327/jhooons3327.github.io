About

I am a PhD student in Biomedical Engineering at the University of Ulsan College of Medicine and a researcher at Asan Medical Center, Seoul, working on deep learning for medical imaging. Most of my work has been in cardiac imaging.

My current work centers on three problems: CT harmonization — pushing kernel conversion toward a fully unpaired setting, with no matched multi-kernel scans; 4D flow MRI reconstruction under high acceleration; and 4D segmentation of time-resolved cardiovascular imaging.
